The Red Story

Red that turns heads, genetics that explain why.

A deep red Toy Poodle is one of those dogs people remember. The coat glows, the eyes pop, and the whole dog has this confident "I know I'm cute" energy. But true red is not a paint job — it is genetics, lineage, and careful selection over time.

Red is often described as one of the newer recognized Poodle colors, becoming official in 1980. Consistent, rich reds are hard to reproduce predictably: coat depth varies because multiple genetic factors influence how intense the red looks and how it matures.

  • Newborn to early weeks — many red puppies are born rich and saturated.
  • 6 to 10 weeks — some lines show a lighter phase, then a darker band near the skin.
  • Teenage & adult coat — some reds deepen again, some soften. Every dog is an individual.

— our promise: we'll always tell you what we see, what the lineage suggests, and what's realistic.

Health Guarantee

Because puppies are loved, not sold.

Every puppy leaves with a written contract and a clear health guarantee. At placement your puppy is certified by a veterinarian as being in good health, with age-appropriate vaccinations, deworming, and a full health record.

Covers

  • Serious genetic or congenital conditions present at birth
  • Confirmed by a licensed veterinarian
  • Documented with supporting veterinary records

Does not cover

  • Routine or minor conditions, common and treatable
  • Accidents, infections, or injuries after placement
  • Conditions from lack of preventive care
Frequently Asked Questions

For curious families starting their Toy Poodle journey.

Availability

Do you always have puppies available?

Not always. Responsible breeding takes time and respect for each dog's health. Puppies are posted on our Available Puppies page once old enough to be assessed.

How does your waitlist work?

We notify people on the list about upcoming litters and expected timelines so you never miss a chance, and we can match puppies thoughtfully rather than rushing placements.

Breed & Color

Will my red Poodle stay red?

Red is a complex expression within the Toy Poodle coat family. Some puppies hold deep red into adulthood and others lighten slightly — both are normal.

Do Toy Poodles shed?

Toy Poodles have a curly coat with minimal shedding, which many find easier on allergies. Regular grooming maintains coat health.

Health

What health testing do you do on parents?

All recommended breed health tests for Toy Poodles, including core eye exams, DNA testing, and joint evaluations — documentation shared openly before placement.

Do puppies come with a health guarantee?

Yes. Every puppy leaves with a written health guarantee and contract explaining coverage and how to activate it.

Red Flags to Watch For

What should I be cautious of in a breeder?

Puppies always available with no waitlist, avoidance of health testing transparency, rushed or defensive communication, or "teacup" labels used as marketing ploys.
